사진 2장만으로 장소가 자연스럽게 바뀌는 공간 전환 영상 제작법을 공유합니다.

- 🚀 빠르게 만들기 → Higgsfield APP Transition
- 🎯 뭉개짐 최소화 + 원하는 동작까지 제어하기 → Kling

저는 Higgsfield로 먼저 해보고, 결과가 아쉬우면 Kling에서 프롬프트를 추가해 다시 제작했어요!

## 01. Jump Transition

<div class="pair"><img src="@/01.jpg" alt="시작 프레임" loading="lazy"><img src="@/02.jpg" alt="끝 프레임" loading="lazy"></div>

### 방법 1. Higgsfield

1. Higgsfield → <b>APPs → Transitions</b> 접속
2. Start Frame / End Frame에 원하는 사진 첨부
3. <b>Jump Transition</b> 선택 후 Generate

### 방법 2. Kling (방법 1이 잘 안 나오는 경우)

1. Higgsfield → <b>Video → Kling 3.0</b> 선택
2. Start Frame / End Frame에 원하는 사진 첨부
3. 아래 프롬프트 입력 후 Generate

```txt
A person jumps powerfully and bursts through the ceiling of a home interior.

As they break through, the ceiling transitions into open sky above an airport.

The subject soars upward into the airport sky, then drops down and lands forcefully on the airport terminal floor below.

Static eye-level camera.

Subject face and body sharp throughout.
```

## 03. Tracking Transition

<div class="pair"><img src="@/05.jpg" alt="시작 프레임" loading="lazy"><img src="@/06.jpg" alt="끝 프레임" loading="lazy"></div>

1. Higgsfield → <b>Video → Kling 3.0</b> 선택
2. Start Frame / End Frame에 원하는 사진 첨부
3. 아래 프롬프트 입력 후 Generate

```txt
A person sits at a sunny terrace cafe.

The person rises from their seat and walks out of frame to the right.

Camera pans right following them.

Mid-pan, the background transitions naturally from the cafe terrace to the banks of the Seine River with the Eiffel Tower ahead.

The person walks back into frame on the right side in the new location.

The person slows down, stops, and strikes a natural pose.

Continuous smooth camera pan.

No face distortion.

Subject body remains sharp throughout.
```

## 04. Stranger Transition

<div class="pair"><img src="@/07.jpg" alt="시작 프레임" loading="lazy"><img src="@/08.jpg" alt="끝 프레임" loading="lazy"></div>

1. Higgsfield → <b>Video → Kling 3.0</b> 선택
2. Start Frame / End Frame에 원하는 사진 첨부
3. 아래 프롬프트 입력 후 Generate

```txt
A person poses in front of the Eiffel Tower.

A passerby quickly walks across the frame very close to the camera, their body wiping across the entire frame from left to right without any blackout or fade.

As the passerby's body sweeps across, the background transitions in the same motion directly from the Eiffel Tower plaza to a Parisian street lined with outdoor book stalls.

No dark transition. No cut. No fade to black.

The wipe is seamless and instant within the passerby's movement.

The person is now browsing books along the street.

One continuous uncut shot.

Same person visible throughout.

No face distortion.

Subject sharp in every frame.
```
